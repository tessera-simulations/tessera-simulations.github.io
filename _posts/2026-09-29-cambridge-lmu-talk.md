---
title: Tessera at the Cambridge–LMU Cosmology Meeting
---

Chris Lovell presented Tessera at the Cambridge–LMU Cosmology Meeting on 29 September 2026, covering the composite zoom approach, the Quijote proof of concept and the first emulated halo and galaxy statistics from the suite.

The talk also showed early results on emulating the two-point correlation function from zoom regions alone, validated on Quijote using 0.1 per cent of the box volume, and the planned extensions: hydrodynamic zooms with multi-fidelity training, cross-over with the DREAMS simulations and field-level emulation.
