---
title: Proof of concept on arXiv
---

The paper that formalises composite zooms as an emulation problem is on arXiv as [2604.17981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17981). Using small regions drawn from the Quijote Big Sobol Sequence, a conditional normalising flow for the halo mass function, conditioned on the overdensity percentile of each region and the cosmology of its parent, recovers the global mass function of the full (1 Gpc/h)³ box using 0.026 per cent of the simulated volume.

That work ran entirely within existing periodic boxes, so the method was validated but the resolution gain that motivates it was not realised. Tessera is the realisation of that gain.
