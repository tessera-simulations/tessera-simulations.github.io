---
title: The Tessera website is live
---

This site is the public home of the Tessera project. It describes the [method](/about/), the [specification of the suite](/simulations/), the [emulators](/emulators/) built on it and the [data products](/data/) we will release with the suite paper.

The suite paper, presenting the design, the particle-mesh parents, region selection, the SWIFT zooms and emulators for the halo mass function and the concentration–mass relation, is in preparation. Until it appears, the catalogues are available to collaborators on request.
