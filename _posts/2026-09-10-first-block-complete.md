---
title: The first 1,024 zooms are complete
---

All 1,024 zooms of the leading power-of-two block of the design have finished on COSMA, together with 17 from the following block. Each has been post-processed with HBT-HERONS, DHalo merger trees, clean-region masks, SHARK galaxies and Synthesizer photometry.

With the first block in hand we retrained the halo mass function emulator on 833 training regions and tested it on 104 held-out regions. The stacked ratio of measured to emulated counts lies between 0.97 and 1.03 in every bin up to 10¹³·⁴ M<sub>☉</sub>/h, and the residuals follow the Poisson expectation across cosmology, environment and mass. The next block of 1,024 zooms is now running.
